Data Residency

Your data is stored on cloud servers in Singapore and the Asia-Pacific region, in compliance with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA). We do not use your data to train any AI models.

What We Collect

We adhere to the principle of data minimization and only collect information necessary to provide the service: registered email, account name, and API usage data. We do not collect unnecessary personal information.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to: provide and improve the service, billing and usage statistics, security protection, and anti-abuse. We never use your API business data for model training or other commercial purposes.

Data Security

We use industry-leading encryption (AES-256) and security measures to protect your data. API Keys are encrypted at rest, all transmissions are protected by TLS, and audit logs are retained for 7 years.

Cross-Border Data Transfer

To provide AI API routing services, your API request content may be transmitted to upstream AI service providers located in other countries/regions. We ensure such transfers comply with the PDPA requirements regarding cross-border data transfers.

Your Rights

Under the Singapore PDPA, you may at any time: access and export your personal data, request correction of inaccurate data, request deletion of your account and data, and withdraw consent for data collection. For questions, please contact support@woodgit.com.
