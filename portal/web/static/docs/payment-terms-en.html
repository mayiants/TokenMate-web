Important Notes

  • Please ensure your account has sufficient funds before making a transaction.
  • If you encounter issues during recharge, please confirm with the relevant third-party payment platform.
  • To protect your account security, you may recharge up to 30 times per month. Unpaid orders will be automatically closed after 1 day.
  • After recharge, you can view your account balance on the Dashboard page.

1. Nature of Recharge

This platform uses a pre-paid consumption model. You must top up your account balance before using API services. Recharge amounts are credited to your account balance and used to offset API usage charges. Account balance is denominated in US Dollars (USD), can only be used for API service consumption on this platform, and has no cash value.

2. Billing Method

Billing is based on token usage, with pricing varying by model and request type (input/output). Token usage per request is determined by the upstream service provider. Cost = input tokens × input unit price + output tokens × output unit price. Charges are deducted in real time after each request; requests will be rejected if the balance is insufficient.

3. Balance Management

Recharge balance does not expire. However, if an account has no login or usage activity for 12 consecutive months, we reserve the right to freeze the account. Account balance is non-transferable.

4. Refund Policy

Recharge balances are generally non-refundable. Refunds may be requested under the following circumstances:

  • We terminate the service for reasons attributable to us, refunding the remaining balance after deducting consumed amounts.
  • Service cannot continue due to force majeure such as changes in applicable laws or termination of upstream services.
  • We decide to terminate services based on business needs or risk assessment.
  • Applicable laws require a mandatory refund.

Refund amount = recharge balance − consumed amount − payment channel fees. Refunds will be returned via the original payment method within 7–15 business days after approval.

5. Redemption Codes

Redemption codes can be entered on the recharge page and exchanged for account balance. Each code is single-use and becomes invalid after redemption. Codes are non-transferable, cannot be exchanged for cash, and are non-refundable. Trading redemption codes is strictly prohibited; violators will have their accounts frozen.

6. Exception Handling

If your balance does not arrive after recharge, please keep the payment receipt and contact support. We will resolve the issue within 1 business day. Billing disputes must be raised within 7 days of the transaction. Duplicate charges caused by system errors will be automatically refunded after verification.

7. Compliance Statement

This platform is operated by a company registered in Singapore. Recharge and payment services comply with the laws of the Republic of Singapore and applicable regulations in the jurisdictions where our services are provided. We do not misappropriate user funds in any form. All transaction records are retained for 7 years for audit purposes.

8. Invoice Policy

Invoice requests are reviewed and issued by administrators, with a processing time of approximately 1–3 business days.
